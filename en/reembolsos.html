Refund Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Each letter is personalized and printed for your order, and sent by physical mail. That's why cancellations and refunds depend on timing:

WhenCan it be refunded?
Before the letter goes to print (for example, while it's under review)Yes, full refund
Already printing or mailedNo, the letter has been produced or is on its way
Duplicate charge or technical errorYes, we fix it or refund it
Order rejected because of the letter's contentYes, full refund

1. How to ask for a refund or cancellation

Email us as soon as possible at hola@cartaparasanta.com with your order number (it starts with PN-). Since the letter goes to print shortly after payment, the sooner you write, the more likely we can cancel it.

2. Address errors

We verify the address with the postal service before charging, but if the letter doesn't arrive because the address given was wrong or incomplete, we can't refund the printing and postage already done. We'll gladly reprint and resend it to the correct address for a fee that covers only printing and postage.

3. Letters that don't arrive or arrive damaged

If the letter is lost in the mail or arrives damaged, email us. We'll check the tracking and resend it at no cost or refund you, your choice.

4. Refund timing

Approved refunds are made through Stripe to the same card and can take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on your bank.
