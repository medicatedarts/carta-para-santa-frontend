Questions from parents

Everything you need to know

What do we get in the mail?

Santa's reply letter, personalized with your child's name and wish list, plus a printed copy of the letter they wrote.

Is the envelope decorated?

No. The envelope on this page is just an illustration. The letter arrives in a standard white window envelope; the magic is inside: Santa's letter is fully designed and decorated.

Can I send letters for several siblings?

Yes. Before paying, tap “Add a letter for another child” and each one writes their own. The first costs $9.99 and each extra letter in the same order $4.99 (up to 4 letters). Each child gets their own envelope with their name, at the same address.

What information about my child do you keep?

Only what's in the letter. Name and age are optional, we never ask for photos, and a grown-up provides the address and pays. We delete the letter content and the address 45 days after delivery. More details in our Privacy Policy.

How long does it take?

4 to 7 business days after payment.

Can I review the letter before paying?

Yes. Before paying you'll see the design, the full text, the address and the final price.

How do I pay? Is it secure?

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
