Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

At ReplyFromSanta.com ("we") children write a letter to Santa and a grown-up buys it so a printed reply arrives at home by mail. This policy explains what information we collect, what we use it for, who we share it with and how long we keep it. Because the site is meant for children, we designed it to collect as little as possible.

1. What we collect

  • From the child's letter: the chosen design, greeting, message, wish list, closing and signature. Name and age are optional. We never ask for the child's photo, school, phone number or email.
  • From the grown-up who buys: name, email, the mailing address, the name for the envelope, and the IP address from which they give consent.
  • Payment: processed by Stripe. We never see or store the card number; we only receive confirmation of the payment.

While the child writes, the letter is saved only in their browser (the device's local storage). Nothing is sent to our servers until the grown-up taps "Pay".

2. Children and parental consent (COPPA)

A child cannot send information on their own: an order is only completed when an adult confirms they are the child's parent or guardian (or have their permission), provides the address and pays with their card. We use the letter's content only to print and mail Santa's reply. We don't use it for advertising, we don't build profiles and we don't sell it.

3. How we use the information

  • To print Santa's personalized reply and the copy of the letter, and mail them.
  • To check with the postal service that the address exists before charging.
  • To send the grown-up the order confirmation and shipping updates.
  • If payment isn't completed, to send the grown-up at most two email reminders (after one hour and about 20 hours later) with a link to finish. Each reminder includes a link to stop receiving them. They are never sent to the child and never include the letter's content.
  • To automatically check that the letter is appropriate (for example, blocking links, phone numbers or offensive language).
  • To handle questions, refunds and shipping issues.

5. Cookies and analytics

We don't use advertising cookies, social media pixels or Google Analytics: our pages contain no code from Meta or other networks. The site only stores in your browser the letter draft (so it isn't lost if you reload), the checkout form data in that same tab and, if you came from a Meta ad, that click's identifier for 90 days. That identifier only leaves your browser if a grown-up buys a letter. You can clear all of this in your browser settings.

6. How long we keep information

If payment isn't completed, we automatically delete the whole order (the letter, the address and the grown-up's details) after 24 hours. If you ask not to receive reminders, we keep only an encrypted fingerprint (hash) of your email to remember your choice.

For paid orders, we automatically delete the letter's content (including the child's name and age) and the mailing address 45 days after the mail delivers the letter (or 65 days after mailing if delivery isn't confirmed). We keep the order number, the grown-up's email and the amount paid for accounting and tax purposes.

7. Your rights and how to ask for deletion

As a parent or guardian you can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your order's information. Email hola@cartaparasanta.com from the address you used to order and include the order number (it starts with PN-). We'll reply within 10 business days. If the letter hasn't been printed yet, deleting it means canceling the order.

8. Security

Connections to the site use HTTPS. Access to orders is limited to the service operator, and system logs don't store letter content or full addresses.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page with its date.
